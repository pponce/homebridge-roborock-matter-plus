## Replacement PR description

Find and Routine switches now acknowledge the press immediately and run the command in the background. Failures remain visible as warnings/errors naming the action, robot and reason. A button acknowledgement means accepted, not that the robot acted.

Start, Dock, Empty Bin and Pause already dispatch in the background on v3.39.0; their changes here are wording.

A repeated press of the same Routine is ignored while its request is queued or in flight, with an informational log. The 1.5-second switch reset does not release this guard. Another press is allowed after the request finishes; physical cleaning completion is not inferred.

Based directly on v3.39.0. Schedule display, MQTT recovery, changelog edits and the TCP-probe fix are excluded.

Physical validation remains pending on this isolated PR: Find and one Routine, each once succeeding and once failing, recording the Apple Home result, physical result and corresponding log lines. Also observe repeated Routine presses while the first request remains pending. Keep draft until the requested physical evidence is recorded.
